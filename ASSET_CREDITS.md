# Environment Asset Credits

The open-world lobby uses public models from the Roblox Creator Store. Models are loaded as visual geometry only. All scripts, tools, remotes, prompts, and click detectors included in imported models are removed before the model enters Workspace.

| Use | Asset | Creator | Creator Store |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open-world village and fortress | Tower Defense Map Strategy Battle Fortress | Creator Store author listed on asset page | https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/128038398267407/Tower-Defense-Map-Strategy-Battle-Fortress-Ga |

Third-party models require **Allow Loading Third Party Assets** in the Roblox experience settings when loaded through `AssetService:LoadAssetAsync()`.
